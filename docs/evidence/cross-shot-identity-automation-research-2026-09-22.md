# Automating cross-shot identity review in All-22 footage

Research checked: 2026-09-22. This is a source-backed design proposal, not a result on the Lions–Rams footage. It extends the [2026-09-15 research note](cross-shot-identity-research-2026-09-15.md) and the [review contract](../human-review-all22.md).

## Recommendation

Automate **candidate generation and review ordering** first. Keep shot-local tracks, synchronized play time, calibrated ground positions, jersey evidence, and visual appearance as separate, auditable signals. Use a constrained assignment with an unmatched outcome; give a global identity only when independent signals agree and the link survives competing-candidate and component checks. Reviewers must establish evaluation truth independently of the proposals. This preserves the repository's abstention policy and permits honest measurement of both labor saved and false merges. See the [existing replay design](../../src/football_tracking/replay.py), [assignment and component guards](../../src/football_tracking/identity.py), and [evaluation plan](../evaluation-plan.md).

This is largely an integration and data problem. The pipeline already has PTS-based replay mapping, field-space candidate evidence, one-to-one assignment with unmatched dummies, and same-shot component guards. At the time of this research snapshot, the [CVAT workflow](../annotation-and-data-generation.md) described import/export adapters as future work, and the run path did not consume OCR or appearance cues. The implementation below adds these paths without treating proposals as truth. No source-video benchmark, CVAT deployment, or model transfer was run for this note. See [CLI construction](../../src/football_tracking/cli.py), [replay scoring](../../src/football_tracking/replay.py), and [identity resolution](../../src/football_tracking/identity_resolution.py).

## New and directly relevant prior art

| Primary source | Method and reported domain | Transfer to this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [FieldMOT, CVPRW 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025W/CVSPORTS/papers/Chen_FieldMOT_A_Field-Registered_Multi-Object_Tracking_for_Sports_Videos_CVPRW_2025_paper.pdf) | Registers detections into field coordinates **before** association, instead of projecting image-space tracks afterward. Its camera-switching sports experiment uses synthetic soccer-field footage; its other test uses a street-view simulation. | A strong architectural ablation: associate aligned ground-contact observations in yards while keeping image-space appearance. Its reported IDF1 improvement cannot be treated as NFL replay performance, and registration failure must produce abstention. |
| [Global ID Fusion and MuPNIT, WACV 2026](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/papers/Wojtulewicz_Advancing_Player_Identification_and_Tracking_with_Global_ID_Fusion_GIF_WACV_2026_paper.pdf) and [authors' code](https://github.com/Wojak27/GIF) | Fuses contextual appearance, motion, and identity memory across camera changes, introducing a multi-perspective **NBA** broadcast benchmark. Its reported gains are on that benchmark. | Study its long-gap memory and global-ID evaluation, then test against a play-scoped NFL baseline. Basketball's scale, uniforms, camera coverage, and substitutions differ; the paper is no validation of zero-false-merge NFL links. |
| [Hand Held Multi-Object Tracking Dataset in American Football, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.09455) | University American football from synchronized handheld wide and tight views, annotated with CVAT. In the authors' BoT-SORT ablation, detector fine-tuning supplied most of the tracking gain (+15.3 HOTA without ReID fine-tuning); ReID-only fine-tuning supplied +0.3 HOTA, and fine-tuning OSNet with a fixed detector gave +0.8 HOTA/+0.8 IDF1. | Useful candidate for local detection, track purity, and ReID stress tests after checking rights and viewpoint similarity. These are MOT results on that dataset, not measured replay cross-shot matching; they argue against assuming a large return from ReID training before fixing detection and geometry. |
| [Jersey recognition framework, CVPRW 2024](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024W/CVsports/papers/Koshkina_A_General_Framework_for_Jersey_Number_Recognition_in_Sports_Video_CVPRW_2024_paper.pdf) and [uncertainty-aware jersey recognition, CVPRW 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025W/CVSPORTS/papers/Grad_Single-Stage_Uncertainty-Aware_Jersey_Number_Recognition_in_Soccer_CVPRW_2025_paper.pdf) | Filter legible crops, combine frame predictions over a tracklet, and model uncertainty or abstention. Experiments are hockey and soccer. | Add per-crop quality and a tracklet-level number distribution. Test on NFL helmet/shoulder-pad occlusion and small All-22 crops before assigning weight. An unreadable or inconsistent number remains unknown. |
| [Homography Based Player Identification in Live Sports, CVPRW 2023](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023W/CVSports/papers/Pandya_Homography_Based_Player_Identification_in_Live_Sports_CVPRW_2023_paper.pdf) | Identifies NFL players by synchronizing helmet video with **external RFID/Next Gen Stats identity tracks** and field registration. | The clearest NFL geometric precedent, but its labeled position feed is an extra input. If an authorized, synchronized tracking feed becomes available, use it as a separate benchmark/input path, never imply video alone produced those IDs. |

The [2026-09-15 note](cross-shot-identity-research-2026-09-15.md) also covers camera calibration (TVCalib, BroadTrack), SoccerNet game-state reconstruction, and the NFL helmet-assignment competition. Those remain useful comparisons. The new work strengthens the case for geometry inside association and for calibrated, multi-cue identity memory; it does not remove the need to measure calibration, timing, track purity, and false merges on this recording.

## Prioritized automation ladder

1. **Make source addressing lossless.** Use the existing [video PTS reader](../../src/football_tracking/video.py) to index `source_sha256`, original zero-based `source_frame`, integer source `pts`, and `time_base`. Each cropped CVAT task or extracted-frame task needs an explicit `task_frame -> source_frame -> source_pts` map; CVAT frame indexes are task-local, and its image-task SDK can return the actual file/frame mapping. Reject missing, duplicated, or conflicting mappings on import. Preserve original 1920×1080 box coordinates, including the crop transform if a review task uses a crop. The repository's [annotation schema](../annotation-schema.md) requires source frame and PTS. [CVAT task SDK examples](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/api_sdk/sdk/examples/tasks/) document task-local frame indexes and image-task file mapping; [FFprobe documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/ffprobe.html) specifies frame-entry extraction. This mapping is a **proposed adapter contract**, not a claim that CVAT exports source PTS itself.
2. **Propose shots, plays, and timing.** Use existing cut proposals and play inventory to generate review queues; let reviewers confirm every cut and play. Candidate `pre_snap`, `snap`, and `ball_release` events may come from video models or a lightweight classifier, but store confidence and original PTS, then validate a piecewise PTS-to-play-time map on held-out events. Flag slow motion, freezes, and edits. Do not turn consecutive broadcast time into presumed continuous play time. See [replay mapping](../../src/football_tracking/replay.py) and the [review contract](../human-review-all22.md).
3. **Propose calibration and contact points.** Detect semantic NFL field markings at sparse camera-motion keyframes, fit on at least four points, and test an independent withheld point. Between reviewed keyframes, propagate field-only motion inside bounded intervals; invalidate when support is poor. Propose player ground contact from feet or box bottom only with a quality flag. Keep the canonical `x=0..120`, `y=0..53⅓` yard field and the existing withheld median ≤1 yard, p95 ≤2 yards gate. FieldMOT supports using field coordinates during association, but its trained soccer keypoints need NFL adaptation. See [calibration timeline](../../src/football_tracking/calibration_timeline.py) and [FieldMOT](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025W/CVSPORTS/papers/Chen_FieldMOT_A_Field-Registered_Multi-Object_Tracking_for_Sports_Videos_CVPRW_2025_paper.pdf).
4. **Preannotate and repair local tracks.** Import detector/tracker boxes as proposal tracks into CVAT, with source tracklet ID, score, team suggestion, and provenance attributes. Review first/last boxes, occlusion and crossing frames, track splits, outside states, and reviewed empty/ignored frames. CVAT's [native video format](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/dataset_management/formats/format-cvat/) represents tracks, keyframes, and `outside`; the [SDK](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/api_sdk/sdk/examples/tasks/) supports annotation import/export. Its [auto-annotation API](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/api_sdk/sdk/auto-annotation/) can run a user-controlled detector and describes a tracker function protocol. Verify the chosen Community deployment path locally before depending on a particular model integration; the [published SAM2 tracker guide](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/annotation/auto-annotation/segment-anything-2-tracker/) currently names Online/Enterprise availability. Local inference proposals remain `reviewed: false` until a person inspects them.
5. **Generate multi-cue cross-shot candidates.** For each reviewed play and valid synchronized overlap, compare team, several calibrated positions and velocities, heading/trajectory shape, jersey-number distributions, and appearance embeddings from high-quality crops. Gate impossible time/field transitions, conflicting teams or reliable numbers, insufficient overlap, uncertain ground contact, and wrong camera calibration before scoring. Normalize distances by timing, contact, and calibration uncertainty; extend the existing [`identity-links.json` candidate evidence](../../src/football_tracking/identity_resolution.py) with signal values and missingness needed for review. Test a field-first association ablation against the current track-then-project resolver. Use learned ReID as an optional ranker until it proves incremental value on held-out NFL pairs; shared uniforms can make generic appearance misleading. See [FieldMOT](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025W/CVSPORTS/papers/Chen_FieldMOT_A_Field-Registered_Multi-Object_Tracking_for_Sports_Videos_CVPRW_2025_paper.pdf), [GIF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/papers/Wojtulewicz_Advancing_Player_Identification_and_Tracking_with_Global_ID_Fusion_GIF_WACV_2026_paper.pdf), and the [jersey studies](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024W/CVsports/papers/Koshkina_A_General_Framework_for_Jersey_Number_Recognition_in_Sports_Video_CVPRW_2024_paper.pdf).
6. **Resolve conservatively and route hard cases.** Run the existing one-to-one assignment with explicit unmatched choices, then apply component-wide same-shot and contradictory-jersey guards. Queue low-margin top-two matches, conflicts, long gaps, and uncertain/missing cues for synchronized side-by-side review. A reviewer records `global_id`, `unknown`, or `ambiguous` with the supporting evidence. The review UI should display both source frame/PTS and common play time; never silently promote a proposal into reference truth. See [identity assignment](../../src/football_tracking/identity.py) and [play-scoped resolution](../../src/football_tracking/identity_resolution.py).

## Measurement before promotion

Keep all views of a play in one split; freeze models and thresholds before test plays. Report (a) reviewed human minutes per play/frame and proposal acceptance/correction rates, (b) calibration and held-out timing errors plus evaluable coverage, (c) local track purity and fragmentation, and (d) correct, incorrect, missed, ambiguous, and abstained cross-shot links, including final component contamination. Compare geometry-only, jersey, ReID, field-first tracking, and combined variants on the **same** reviewed plays and detections. Use independent review labels, not model-suggested links, as reference. Retain the [readiness audit](../../scripts/check_identity_readiness.py) as the promotion gate; `ready_for_evaluation` cannot be inferred from this research or a 12-frame scouting pack. See [evaluation plan](../evaluation-plan.md) and [review instructions](../human-review-all22.md).

The first implementation slice is the source-frame/PTS-preserving CVAT import/export adapter and a review queue built from existing `identity-links.json` evidence. This unlocks annotation acceleration without weakening the readiness gate. Follow with the field-first and OCR/ReID ablations only after there are independently reviewed play pairs to score.

## Implementation status

Implemented in this repository: a CVAT XML exporter/importer with source-hashed frame/PTS/crop mapping; player boxes, ground-contact points, and optional field-intersection point proposals; unreviewed adjacent-shot play windows and bounded motion-burst/camera-motion proposals; source-validated identity review queues; local Tesseract jersey proposals; and optional source/base-analysis-bound jersey and appearance cues. A reviewed `global_id` now requires an independent second-review record. Small generated-video round-trips exercised these interfaces, including point projection through a validated calibration timeline. The supplied full-game footage has not been labeled or scored by these tools, and readiness remains gated on reviewed source annotations, timing, calibration, and identity labels.
